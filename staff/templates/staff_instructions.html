dashboard Staff Dashboard

The Staff Dashboard is your home screen. It shows live portfolio statistics for all loans in the system and quick-access cards to the most common tasks.

Summary Cards
  • Total Loans – all loan records regardless of status.
  • Running – active loans with repayments in progress.
  • Pending – applications submitted by clients awaiting review.
  • Defaulted – loans with overdue repayments beyond the grace period.
  • Completed – fully repaid and closed loans.
Navigation

Use the left sidebar to access all staff functions:

  • Clients – view and manage client profiles.
  • Loans – view and manage the loan pipeline.
  • Transactions – view payment history across all loans.
  • Messages – send or view messages.
  • Support Tickets – manage client support requests.
  • Credit Rating – assess a client's credit standing.
  • SME Profiles – manage SME loan applicant profiles.
  • Existing Loan Functions – add loans/statements for existing clients, in bulk or one at a time (see sidebar).
  • Client Credits – track and refund client overpayments (see Loan Credits & Refunds).
  • Alesco Update – process government payroll deduction files.
Switching Dashboards

The top navigation bar has buttons to switch between Admin, Staff, and User dashboards if you have access to multiple roles.

people Managing Clients

The Clients section lists every registered borrower. You can search, view, add, and update their profiles and documents.

Viewing the Client List
1
Click Clients in the sidebar. The table shows all registered clients with name, employer, status, and loan count.
2
Use the search bar or column filters to find a client by name, employer, or ID number.
3
Click View to open the client's full profile page.
Client Profile Sections
  • Personal – name, date of birth, gender, NID number, marital status, place of origin.
  • Contact – email, work email, mobile numbers, work phone.
  • Employment – employer, job title, work ID, office address, start date.
  • Banking – bank, branch, account name, account number.
  • Residential – current address.
  • Loans – all loan records tied to this client with status badges.
  • Documents – uploaded supporting files (payslips, ID, signed forms).
Editing a Client Profile
  • Each section on the profile page has an Edit button. Click it, update the relevant fields, and save.
  • Changes take effect immediately and are reflected in all future PDF documents generated for this client.
Adding a New Client
1
Click Clients → Add Client (or the Add User button on the clients list).
2
Fill in the client's email address and set an initial password. The client can change their password after first login.
3
Open the new client's profile and complete all personal, employment, and banking sections before creating a loan.
Employer & Payroll Officer Details
  • The Employer tab on the profile wizard has two modes depending on system settings:
    • Preregistration ON – only an employer dropdown is shown. Select the employer and the system automatically saves the payroll officer name, phone, and email from the employer record. You do not need to enter these fields manually.
    • Preregistration OFF – all employer and payroll officer fields are shown as editable inputs. Fill in all fields manually.
  • Work Email is the client's own personal work email (unique per client). Payroll Officer Email is the shared payroll section email for the client's employer — all clients from the same employer share the same payroll officer contact.
Bank Account – Branch Filtering
  • On the Bank Account tab, selecting a Bank from the dropdown automatically filters the Bank Branch list to show only branches belonging to that bank.
  • If you change the bank, the branch field resets — re-select the correct branch for the new bank.
Uploading Client Documents
  • On the client profile, each document section (Payslips, ID, Bank Statement, etc.) has an Upload button.
  • Documents should be uploaded as PDF or image files. Maximum file size is typically 5 MB per file.
  • Uploaded documents are required before a loan can proceed beyond the Documents Pending stage.
Tip: Always complete the client's profile fully — including employer and banking details — before submitting a loan application. Incomplete profiles cause delays in the approval process.
account_balance Managing Loans

The Loans section is where you track the full lifecycle of every loan — from application through to completion.

Loan Status Flow
  • Awaiting T&C – client has submitted an application; documents are being prepared.
  • Under Review – application submitted for admin review.
  • On Hold – application paused pending additional information.
  • Approved – approved by admin; awaiting disbursement.
  • Running – loan disbursed and repayments active.
  • Defaulted – one or more repayments overdue beyond the grace period.
  • Completed – all repayments received; loan fully settled.
Viewing Loan Lists
  • Loans → All – full loan list with search and filter (includes an Awaiting Refund tab — see Loan Credits & Refunds in the sidebar).
  • Loans → Pending – applications awaiting review.
  • Loans → Under Review – loans in the review stage.
  • Loans → Unfinished – incomplete applications not yet submitted.
Loan Amount Limits & Apply Advance to Missed Payments NEW
  • Every loan amount you enter — whether creating a loan, an additional loan, or an existing-loan-with-details — is checked against the admin's configured minimum/maximum (or a personal override on the client's profile). An amount outside the limit is rejected immediately with a clear message.
  • If a client has a prepaid Advance Balance large enough to cover a missed repayment, Create Default is blocked — use Apply Advance to Missed Payments on the loan page instead. It settles the repayment straight from the advance with no default charged.
Viewing a Loan
1
Click View Loan on any loan row to open the full loan detail page.
2
Review the loan summary: amount, repayment amount, number of fortnights, start and end dates.
3
Check the Statement tab for the full transaction history.
4
Check the Documents section to confirm all signed documents have been uploaded.
Submitting a Loan for Review
1
Ensure the client profile is fully filled out and all required documents are uploaded.
2
Open the loan and click Submit for Review. The status changes to Under Review and the admin is notified.
Note: Staff cannot approve or disburse loans — only admins can perform those actions. Your role is to prepare the application and documents to the required standard.
add_circle Creating a Loan

Staff can create a loan application on behalf of a client directly from the staff portal.

Before You Start
  • The client must already have a registered account.
  • Their personal, employment, and banking details must be complete.
  • At least one payslip must be uploaded to their profile.
  • The client must not have an existing active or pending loan — if they do, you will be redirected to the Existing Loan page (see below).
Step-by-Step
1
Go to Loans → Create Loan.
2
Search for the client by name or email and select them.
3
Select the Location.
4
Enter the Loan Amount and Number of Fortnights. The system calculates the repayment amount from the repayment table.
5
Set the Repayment Start Date to the client's next pay date.
6
Click Create Loan. The system generates the loan and sends the application documents (Loan Form, T&C, IRSDA, Stat Dec) to the client's email.
7
Upload the signed documents to the loan file once the client returns them.
8
Click Submit for Review once all documents are in place.
Client Already Has a Loan
  • If you select a client who already has an active or pending loan, you will be redirected to the Existing Loan page instead of the creation form.
  • The page shows the client's current loan: reference, amount, outstanding balance, status, and next payment date.
  • If the client needs additional funds on top of their existing balance, click Get Additional Loan. This opens the Additional Loan form which automatically handles the balance transfer and recalculates repayments — structured according to whichever Refinance Type the admin has configured.
  • A new standalone loan cannot be created while an active or pending loan exists.
  • NEW If the admin has set Refinance Type to Refinance Not Allowed, the Get Additional Loan button won't even appear — a client with any existing loan cannot get a new one under any circumstances until it reaches Completed.
Loan Calculator

The built-in loan calculator lets you test different amounts and terms before committing. Use it to show the client what their repayments would be at various loan amounts.

Uploading Signed Documents
  • On the loan view page, each document type has an upload slot.
  • Upload the signed PDF for: Loan Application, Terms & Conditions, IRSDA, and Statutory Declaration.
  • The document checklist turns green once all four are uploaded.

The system generates pre-filled PDF documents from client and loan data. Staff can download and share these from the loan view page.

Documents You Can Download

  • Terms & Conditions / Loan Agreement – includes all legal clauses with the client's name and loan amounts filled in.
  • IRSDA (Irrevocable Authority to Deduct Salary) – addressed to the client's employer with deduction amounts and schedule.

  • Cash Disbursement Notice (CDN) – generated when a loan is disbursed.
  • Loan Amortization Schedule (LAS) – full projected repayment table showing principal, interest, and balance for every fortnight.

Downloading Documents
1
Open the loan via Loans → View Loan.
2
Scroll to the documents section or use the tab navigation at the top of the loan page.
3
Click the Download button next to the document you need.
Emailing Documents to the Client

When a loan application is first created, all four application documents (Loan Form, T&C, IRSDA, Stat Dec) are automatically emailed to the client. If the client did not receive them, open the loan and click Resend Documents.

Tip: Print the IRSDA and Loan Application and have the client sign them in person when possible — this avoids the back-and-forth of scanning and uploading.
verified Credit Rating

The Credit Rating section lets you assess a client's financial position before creating a loan.

What the Credit Check Shows
  • Total Active Loans – number of currently running loans for this client.
  • Total Outstanding Balance – combined amount still owed across all active loans.
  • Monthly/Fortnightly Commitments – total repayments the client is currently making.
  • Debt-to-Income Ratio – total repayments as a percentage of salary. The system flags this if it exceeds the maximum allowed limit set in Settings.
  • Default History – any loans that have previously entered default status.
Loan Requirements Matrix

The Loan Requirements page (sidebar) shows a checklist of all required documents and profile fields for each loan type. Use this to quickly see what is still missing before submitting a loan for review.

When to Run a Credit Check
  • Always run a credit check before creating a loan for a client who already has active loans.
  • If the debt-to-income ratio is above the allowed limit, do not proceed — the admin will reject the application.

receipt_long Alesco Payroll Integration

Alesco is the government payroll system. The Alesco integration lets you upload the periodic deduction listing file and automatically match deductions to client loans — instead of entering payments one by one.

Uploading an Alesco Deduction File
1
Go to Alesco → Update in the sidebar.
2
Upload the Alesco Deduction Variation Report text file received from the payroll department.
3
The system parses the fixed-layout file, creates a Pay Run record, and matches each deduction line to a client by Employee File Number.
4
Review the confirmation page: it shows how many lines were matched, unmatched, or pending.
Confirming Deduction Lines
  • Confirm a single line: Click Confirm next to a matched line. The system records the payment against the client's loan, creates a statement entry, and updates the loan balance.
  • Confirm all lines: Click Confirm All to batch-confirm every pending matched line in the run. This is the fastest way to process a clean payroll file.
  • Skip a line: If a deduction doesn't match a loan (e.g. the client has no active loan), you can skip it.
Understanding Line Statuses
  • Pending – matched to a client and loan; awaiting your confirmation.
  • Confirmed – payment has been posted to the loan statement; the action button changes to Rollback.
  • Unmatched – the Employee File Number didn't match any client in the system. Check the file number on the client's profile.
  • Skipped – the listing shows a zero deduction for this pay (no money came in), so there is nothing to confirm. The expected repayment still falls due and can be defaulted from the loan page if unpaid.
Cancel Update

On the Confirm page, Cancel Update abandons the whole upload so you can fix and re-upload the file:

  • If nothing was confirmed yet, the upload and its pending lines are simply deleted.
  • If the run was partially confirmed, every confirmed payment is rolled back first (see below) and the pending lines are discarded, then the upload is deleted.
Rollback (wrong upload confirmed)

Every confirmation snapshots the loan first, so it can be reversed exactly:

  • Per line: on the Confirm page, click Rollback next to a confirmed line. The payment and its statement entries are removed and the loan balance, arrears, schedule position and status are restored to exactly what they were before.
  • Whole upload: on the Alesco Update page, the most recent completed/partial upload shows a Rollback button in the Recent Uploads table. It reverses every confirmed payment in that run, discards any pending lines, and removes the upload — a clean slate to re-upload the corrected file.
Safety rule: a line will refuse to roll back if any newer payment or statement was recorded on that loan after the confirmation — rolling back would erase that later work. Those loans are listed in the error message and must be corrected manually. Payments confirmed before rollback support existed have no snapshot and also cannot be auto-reversed.
Categorized Unmatched Payments NEW

A deduction that couldn't be matched to any loan lands on the Unmatched Payment Listing, sorted into a reason so you know what to do next: No Client Record (file number matches nobody), Possible Typo (a very close match exists — one click links it), Ambiguous Match (more than one client shares the number — pick the right one), or Client Has No Loan (the client exists but has nothing to apply the payment to). Use the Link to Client action to fix a file number and re-match a line — it becomes payable immediately on the normal Confirm page.

Important: The Alesco deduction category must be enabled in Admin Settings → General Settings for this feature to be available. If you don't see the Alesco menu item, ask an admin to enable it.
list_alt Transaction Statements

The Transactions/Statements page shows every payment, default, and adjustment across all loans. Use it to investigate payment issues, audit balances, and generate reports.

Viewing All Transactions
1
Click Transactions in the sidebar to open the full transaction list.
2
The table shows: date, loan reference, client name, statement type, debit, credit, and balance for each entry.
3
Summary totals are shown at the top: total payments, total defaults, and total credits.
Filtering Transactions
  • Date range – filter by start and end date to see transactions for a specific period.
  • Loan type – filter by Personal or SME loans.
  • Transaction type – filter by PAYMENT, DEFAULT, or OTHER to isolate specific entry types.
Statement Entry Types
  • PAYMENT – a repayment received (payroll deduction, bank deposit, or cash). Increases credit, reduces balance.
  • DEFAULT – a missed repayment beyond the grace period. Increases debit (adds default interest), increases balance.
  • ADVANCE – an over-payment recorded as advance balance for future arrears allocation.
  • OTHER – adjustments such as advance-balance allocation to arrears or manual corrections.
Loan Requirements Matrix

The Loan Requirements Matrix page shows all pending loans and their document completion status at a glance. Each row shows which required documents have been uploaded and which are still missing. Use it to prioritise which clients to follow up with.

Tip: If a client reports a payment isn't showing, use the Transactions page filtered by their loan reference to verify. Only admins can delete or correct statement entries — escalate to an admin if a correction is needed.
upload_file Existing Loan Functions NEW

Everything for entering a client's existing/historical loan data — or adding a loan for a client the normal way — lives in one place: Existing Loan Functions in the sidebar. This replaced the three separate menu items that used to be here (Add Existing Loan, Add Existing Statements, Add a Loan Statement) with six clearly-explained buttons.

1. Add Existing Loan with Details

Use Add Existing Loan with Details to create a single new loan for one client, entering the full amount, term, and dates by hand — the normal loan-creation form, one client at a time. Blocked if the client already has an active or pending loan (redirects to the Existing Loan page).

2. Add Additional Loan

Use Add Additional Loan when a client with a running loan needs more funds — the system closes the old loan, transfers the outstanding balance, and structures the new combined loan according to the admin's configured Refinance Type. If Refinance Type is set to Refinance Not Allowed, this button will refuse and tell you to wait until the client's existing loan is fully Completed.

3. Upload Files for Existing Loan NEW
1
Click Upload Files for Existing Loan.
2
Search for the loan by reference or client name in the list, then click Upload Files on the correct row.
3
You'll see every document slot for that loan (application form, T&Cs, payslips, bank/super statements, funding receipt). Use Add or Replace next to any slot to upload a file — PDF or images, up to 15 MB.
4. Add Existing Loans in Bulk
1
Click Add Existing Loans in Bulk.
2
Download the loan upload template (Excel). Fill in all required columns: client email, loan amount, repayment amount, start date, status, etc.
3
Upload the completed file. The system creates loan records for each valid row.
5. Add Existing Statements in Bulk
1
Click Add Existing Statements in Bulk.
2
Use the statements template covering many loans at once. Each row should have: Loan Ref, Date, Statement description, Debit or Credit amount.
3
Upload the file. The system posts each transaction to the matching loan statement.
6. Add a Loan Statement
1
Click Add a Loan Statement.
2
Pick one existing loan from the list — this is for entering a single loan's full history, rather than a spreadsheet covering many loans at once.
3
Upload that loan's statement file. Zero-amount rows are recorded as a default; credit-only rows as an adjustment; debit-only rows as a payment.
Warning: Bulk uploads are difficult to reverse. Always check your template data carefully and test with a small batch before uploading the full file. Keep a copy of the source file for your records.
volunteer_activism Loan Credits & Refunds NEW

When a closing payment overpays a loan's balance — most commonly an external refinance payoff for a bit more than was actually owed — the excess is never silently discarded. It's tracked as a credit owed back to the client until it's refunded.

Awaiting Refund Loans
  • A loan like this is marked Awaiting Refund instead of Completed. The debt itself is fully settled — only the refund is still outstanding.
  • The excess amount is recorded against the client in Client Credits (sidebar).
Recording a Refund
1
Go to Client Credits.
2
Find the client in the "Owing to Clients" table.
3
Once you've actually paid them back, use the Mark Refunded form: enter the date, an optional note, and upload the refund receipt.
4
This automatically finalises the loan to Completed if it was still Awaiting Refund — no extra step needed.
Closing the Loan Before the Refund Happens

If you need to close the loan out now and deal with the refund later, open the loan itself — the Awaiting Refund banner has an Attribute to Client Credit & Close Now button. This closes the loan immediately; the credit stays open in Client Credits until refunded whenever.

Good to know: if an admin has set Refinance Type to Refinance Not Allowed, a client with any existing loan cannot get a new or additional loan at all until it reaches Completed — the Add Additional Loan button will refuse with a message telling you why.
schedule Automation & Alerts

Several routine tasks now run automatically in the background on a schedule. Knowing what is automatic saves you doing it by hand — and tells you when manual action is still useful.

What Runs Automatically
  • Repayment reminders are emailed to clients each day before their repayment is due — you do not need to send these manually for routine reminders.
  • Default processing runs every 30 minutes: loans overdue beyond the grace period are flagged as defaulted, default interest is applied, and the client is notified.
  • Default classification (aging/recovery) is refreshed automatically.
  • Payment confirmations and completion notices are emailed to clients automatically when you record a payment that clears a balance.
When to Still Act Manually
  • Use Send Repayment Reminder for an ad-hoc push (e.g. after a public holiday) or to remind a single client from their loan page.
  • Use Run Defaults right after a large batch payment upload, so arrears and balances reflect the new data immediately rather than waiting for the next scheduled run.
Good to know: When you send a bulk message, it is processed in the background so the page returns straight away. If the background worker is briefly unavailable, the system still sends the message within the request — nothing is silently lost.
Login security: After several failed login attempts an account is temporarily blocked for a few minutes. If a client reports being locked out, tell them to wait a few minutes and then use Forgot Password if needed.
support_agent Support Tickets

Staff can view and respond to client support tickets raised through the client portal. Prompt, professional responses help resolve client issues quickly.

Accessing Support Tickets

Click Support Ticket in the sidebar. This shows all tickets in the system — not just the ones assigned to you.

Ticket Statuses
  • Awaiting Reply – client has sent a message and is waiting for a staff response.
  • Replied – a staff or admin member has responded.
  • Closed – the ticket has been resolved.
Viewing a Ticket
1
Click View Thread on any ticket row to open the conversation.
2
The thread shows the full conversation history. Client messages are on the left; staff replies are on the right.
3
Review the ticket category and subject to understand what the client needs help with.
Replying to a Ticket
1
Type your reply in the Reply text box at the bottom of the thread.
2
Click Reply to send. The client receives an email notification with your response and a link to view the ticket.
3
The ticket status automatically changes to Replied.
Closing a Ticket

Once the client's issue is fully resolved, click Close Ticket. The client receives a closure notification. If a ticket was closed prematurely, the admin can reopen it.

Ticket Categories & How to Handle Them
  • Loan Requirements – tell the client what documents or information is needed and how to upload them.
  • Pending Loan – check the loan status and update the client on where it is in the process.
  • Loan Repayment – verify payment records and clarify repayment dates or amounts.
  • Loan Issue – investigate the specific loan, check the statement, and escalate to admin if needed.
  • Account Activation – direct the client to use Forgot Password, or flag to admin to manually activate the account.
  • Web App Usage – guide the client through the feature they are having difficulty with.
Best practice: Always reply using clear, professional language. If you are unsure of the answer, say "I'll look into this and get back to you shortly" rather than leaving the ticket in Awaiting Reply with no response. Escalate complex issues to an admin.
share Referrers & Commission

The referral system lets you record who referred a new client to the company. Once a referrer is linked at client registration, the system automatically calculates and tracks the commission owed to them.

What Is a Referrer?

A referrer is any individual — existing client, community contact, or business partner — who recommends the company's services and brings in a new client. The company pays them a fixed commission per referred client at the end of each month.

How to Assign a Referrer When Creating a Client
1
Go to Clients → Add Client.
2
Fill in the client's personal details as normal.
3
In the Referred By dropdown, select the referrer's name. Only Active referrers appear in this list.
4
If no referrer is applicable, leave the field blank (it is optional).
5
Submit the form. The system automatically creates a referral record and assigns the current commission rate to the referrer. A confirmation message will appear confirming the amount recorded.
What Happens After You Assign a Referrer
  • A referral record is created instantly — you do not need to do anything else.
  • The commission is set at the rate configured in Admin Settings at the time of registration.
  • The admin reviews and pays all outstanding commissions at the end of each month through the Commission Overview page (admin-only).
What If the Referrer Is Not in the List?
  • The referrer must be registered in the system by an admin before they appear in the dropdown.
  • Ask your admin to add the referrer under Referrers → Add Referrer.
  • Do not create the client without selecting the referrer — if you forget, the referral will not be recorded automatically.
What Staff Cannot Do
  • Staff cannot add or edit referrers — that is an admin function.
  • Staff cannot view commission amounts or mark commissions as paid.
  • Staff can only assign a referrer at the time of client creation.
Important: Always ask the client at registration whether they were referred by someone. If you forget to select the referrer when creating the client, contact an admin — they can update the Referred By field on the client's profile directly.

Ask the client to check their spam/junk folder. If nothing is there, open the loan view page and click Resend Documents. Verify the client's email address on their profile is correct before resending.
help Where do I download the Loan Amortization Schedule? expand_more
Open the loan, go to the LAS tab (or look for the Download LAS button on the loan view page), and click download. The PDF will be generated and downloaded immediately.
business SME & Alesco Payroll
help When should I create an SME profile for a client? expand_more
Create an SME profile when the client is applying for a business loan (Sole Trader, SME, or MSME category). The personal profile must already exist. Complete the SME profile — including business details, documents, and SME bank account — before creating the SME loan application.
help An Alesco line shows as "Unmatched" — what do I do? expand_more
This means the Employee File Number in the Alesco file doesn't match any client profile. Open the client's profile, check the Employee File Number field under Job Information, and correct it if it's wrong. Then re-upload the Alesco file, or ask an admin to manually record the payment.
help Can I undo a confirmed Alesco line? expand_more
No — once confirmed, the payment is posted to the loan statement. If a line was confirmed in error, contact an admin; they can delete the statement entry and re-enter it correctly.
help I don't see the Alesco menu item — where is it? expand_more
The Alesco feature is only available when the Alesco Payroll deduction category is enabled in Admin Settings. Ask an admin to turn on ALESCO Payroll under Settings → General Settings → Deduction Category Toggles.
support_agent Support Tickets
help Can I close a ticket if the client has not responded? expand_more
Yes. If you have resolved the issue and the client has not responded after a reasonable time, you can click Close Ticket. The client will receive a closure email. If they re-open the issue later, they can create a new ticket or an admin can reopen the closed one.
help A client's ticket is about something I cannot resolve — what should I do? expand_more
Reply to the ticket to acknowledge receipt and let the client know you are escalating it. Then notify an admin directly. Admins have access to settings, financial adjustments, and account-level actions that staff cannot perform.
help Does the client get notified when I reply? expand_more
Yes. Every reply you post through the ticket system automatically sends an email to the client with your response and a link to view the full thread in their portal.
share Referrers & Commission
help The referrer a client mentioned is not in the dropdown — what do I do? expand_more
The referrer must be registered in the system by an admin before they appear in the list. Ask your admin to add them under Referrers → Add Referrer. Once added and set to Active, they will appear in the dropdown. In the meantime, create the client without selecting a referrer and ask the admin to update the Referred By field on the client's profile afterwards.
help I forgot to select the referrer when I created the client — can it be fixed? expand_more
Yes. Contact an admin and let them know the client's name and the referrer's name. The admin can update the Referred By field on the client's profile. Note that the referral commission record should then be created manually — the admin handles this through the referral management pages.
help Does every new client need a referrer? expand_more
No. The Referred By field is optional. Only fill it in if the client was genuinely referred by someone registered in the system. Leave it blank for walk-in clients, online sign-ups, or any client whose referrer is unknown.
help Can I see how much commission a referrer will earn? expand_more
The commission amount is set by the admin in Referral Settings. When you select a referrer and submit the client form, a confirmation message shows the commission amount that was recorded. Detailed commission tracking and payment management is handled by admins only.
volunteer_activism Loan Credits & Refunds
help A client's loan shows "Awaiting Refund" instead of Completed — what does that mean? expand_more
The closing payment (often an external refinance payoff) paid more than the loan actually owed. The debt is settled, but the excess is still owed back to the client — tracked in Client Credits — so the loan is deliberately kept out of Completed until the refund is handled.
help How do I mark a client's credit as refunded? expand_more
Go to Client Credits in the sidebar, find their row, and use Mark Refunded — enter the date, an optional note, and upload the refund receipt. The loan finalises to Completed automatically if it was still Awaiting Refund.
help The "Get Additional Loan" button has disappeared for a client with an existing loan — why? expand_more
An admin has set Refinance Type to Refinance Not Allowed. While that's active, a client with any existing loan (funded or pending) cannot get a new or additional loan at all — the button is hidden and the action is blocked server-side too. Wait until their existing loan reaches Completed.
