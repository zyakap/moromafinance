Borrowing with your eyes open

How do you decide how much I can borrow?

We look at your income, your existing commitments and your repayment capacity. Your approved amount is the figure we're confident you can comfortably repay — which may be less than you requested.

What information do you collect?

Only what we need to assess and manage your loan: your identity, employment and income details, bank account and supporting documents such as a payslip and ID.

Who can see my information?

Your information is confidential. It is accessed only by authorised Moroma Finance staff for the purpose of assessing and administering your loan, and is never sold.

What if I can't make a repayment?

Contact us as soon as you know. Reach us on 7010 7473 or 8302 9377, or email sales@moromafinance.com.pg. Early contact gives us the most options to help.

What happens if I default?

Missed repayments may lead to your loan being classified in default, which can affect your standing with us and your ability to borrow in future. We will always try to reach an arrangement with you first.
