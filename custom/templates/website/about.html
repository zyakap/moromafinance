01 · Who we are

A Papua New Guinean finance company

Moroma Finance Limited is a Papua New Guinean finance company providing accessible lending services to eligible salaried employees. We support our clients through a branch network and a central Head Office that manages credit assessment, loan processing, approvals, data management and portfolio administration.

We combine local customer service with centralised credit control. Our branches promote the company, receive enquiries and applications, assist clients and maintain local relationships — while Head Office performs the key processing, approval and record-management functions, so every lending decision is consistent and accountable.

  • Personal and payroll-based lending for eligible salaried employees
  • Professional client service from enquiry through repayment completion
  • Accurate loan account maintenance and statement issuance
  • Responsible lending based on affordability, employment and payroll verification

03 · Mission
To provide timely, transparent and customer-focused lending services that support the financial needs of eligible employees while protecting the sustainability of the company.
  • Formal verification and affordability assessment on every application
  • Approval only by authorised Head Office officers
  • Clear product information and transparent loan terms
  • Confidential handling of client, payroll and banking information
