Who we are

A Papua New Guinean finance company

Moroma Finance Limited is a Papua New Guinean finance company providing accessible lending services to eligible salaried employees. We support our clients through a branch network and a central Head Office that manages credit assessment, loan processing, approvals, data management and portfolio administration.

We combine local customer service with centralised credit control. Our branches promote the company, receive enquiries and applications, assist clients and maintain local relationships — while Head Office performs the key processing, approval and record-management functions, so every lending decision is consistent and accountable.

  • Personal and payroll-based lending for eligible salaried employees
  • Professional client service from enquiry through repayment completion
  • Accurate loan account maintenance and statement issuance
  • Responsible lending based on affordability, employment and payroll verification

Who we serve

Lending to Papua New Guinea's workforce

Moroma Finance principally serves eligible salaried employees from public-sector institutions and other approved organisations, including:

  • National government departments and agencies
  • Provincial governments and district administrations
  • Provincial Health Authorities, hospitals and health-sector organisations
  • Education departments, schools and teachers
  • Police, Correctional Services, Defence, Fire Services and other disciplined services
  • Statutory authorities and state-owned enterprises

How we lend responsibly

Every application completes formal verification and affordability assessment by an authorised Head Office officer before approval. We check identity, employment, payroll information and repayment capacity — and we keep your personal, payroll and banking information confidential.

Clear product information, transparent loan terms, accurate statements and reasonable assistance when you raise a concern — that's our customer commitment.
