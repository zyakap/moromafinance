Frequently asked

How do I check my loan balance?

Log in to your Moroma Finance account. Your dashboard shows your current balance, your next repayment and your full repayment schedule.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Use the "forgot password" option on the login page to reset it by email. If you're still locked out, call us on 7010 7473 and we'll help.

Can I pay my loan out early?

Yes. Contact us and we'll confirm your payout figure so you can settle your loan ahead of schedule.

Can I apply for another loan while one is running?

In some cases, yes — it depends on your repayment history and capacity. Trusted clients in good standing have the best chance. Talk to us about your options.

How do I update my bank or contact details?

Log in and edit your profile, or call us and we'll update it for you. Keeping your details current means your funds and notices reach you.

How do I make a complaint?

We want to know. Email sales@moromafinance.com.pg or call 7010 7473 and we'll look into it promptly.
