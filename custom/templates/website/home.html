FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can apply for a Moroma Finance loan?

Employed Papua New Guineans with a regular income can apply. Create an account, complete your profile and we'll assess your eligibility and repayment capacity.

How much can I borrow?

Personal loans range from K500 up to K5,000. Trusted clients with a good repayment history may qualify for higher limits and special offers.

How do repayments work?

Repayments are made in fortnightly instalments aligned to your pay cycle, so they stay manageable. You'll see the full repayment schedule before you accept your loan.

What is the interest rate?

Interest is set by our published Moroma Finance repayment schedule, not by a single flat rate. It depends on how much you borrow and how many fortnights you repay over — the longer the term, the more interest you pay. Use the calculator on our Rates & Fees page to see the exact fortnightly repayment and total repayable for your combination before you apply.

What do I need to apply?

You'll need employment confirmation, two valid IDs (Work ID, NID or Driver's License) and a filled & signed loan application form. Register online and our team will guide you through it.

How long does approval take?

Eligible clients can be approved the same day. Once approved, funds are paid directly into your bank account.
