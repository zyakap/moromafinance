Questions, answered

Who can apply for a Moroma Finance loan?

Eligible salaried employees — principally public-sector staff (departments, PHAs, schools, disciplined services, statutory authorities) and approved private organisations. Create an account, complete your profile and we'll assess your eligibility and repayment capacity.

How much can I borrow?

Personal loans range from K500 up to K5,000 in K50 steps, over 7 to 36 fortnights. Trusted clients with a good repayment history may qualify for higher limits and special offers.

How do repayments work?

Repayments are fortnightly instalments deducted in line with your pay cycle, via payroll deduction or standing order. You'll see the full schedule before you accept your loan.

What is the interest rate?

Interest is set by our published repayment schedule, not a single flat rate. It depends on the amount and the term — use the calculator on our Rates page to see your exact fortnightly repayment before you apply.

What do I need to apply?

A completed application form, two latest payslips, a Pay Variation Advice (PVA), an employment confirmation letter, your work ID and a 2–3 month bank statement. Register online and our team will guide you through it.

How long does approval take?

Eligible clients can be approved the same day. Once approved, funds are paid directly into your bank account.
