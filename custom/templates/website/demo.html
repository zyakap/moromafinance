How to Apply

Your loan, step by step

From registering to receiving your cash — here's exactly what to expect, and what to have ready.

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Create your account

Register with your name, email and phone number. This becomes your Moroma Finance account, where you'll manage everything.

Complete your profile

Add your employment, income and bank details, and upload your ID and a recent payslip. Accurate details mean a faster decision.

Choose your loan

Select how much you need and over how many fortnights. You'll see your repayment and total repayable before you commit.

Get approved

We assess your application and repayment capacity. Eligible clients can be approved the same day.

Receive your cash

Once you accept your offer, funds are paid into your bank account and your repayment schedule begins.

Have these ready

  • A valid photo ID
  • Proof of employment
  • Your most recent payslip
  • Your bank account details
  • A working email & phone number
