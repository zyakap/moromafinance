How our pricing works

Priced from the schedule, not a flat rate

Moroma Finance does not use a single flat rate. Your interest is set by our published Personal Loan Repayment Schedule, based on how much you borrow and how many fortnights you repay over. A shorter term carries less interest; a longer term carries more.

Whatever you choose, the fortnightly repayment and total repayable are agreed with you before you sign.

Check your repayment
Principle / 1

Shown up front

Before you accept, you'll see your loan amount, your fortnightly repayment and your total repayable — in writing.

Principle / 2

Matched to your pay

Repayments are spread across fortnights and matched to your pay cycle, so they stay manageable.

Principle / 3

No hidden charges

What we quote is what you repay. Any fee that ever applies to your loan is disclosed to you before you sign.
