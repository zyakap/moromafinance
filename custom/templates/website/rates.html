How our pricing works

Priced from our repayment schedule

Moroma Finance does not use a single flat rate. Your interest is set by our published Moroma Finance Repayment Schedule, based on how much you borrow and how many fortnights you repay over. A shorter term carries less interest; a longer term carries more. Whatever you choose, the fortnightly repayment and total repayable are agreed with you before you sign.
