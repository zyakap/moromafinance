dashboard Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is the first screen after logging in. It gives a live snapshot of your loan portfolio's health.

Key Metrics Cards
  • Total Loans – total records regardless of status.
  • Active / Running – loans currently being repaid on schedule.
  • Defaulted – loans with missed repayments beyond the grace period.
  • Pending – applications awaiting admin review.
  • Total Disbursed – cumulative amount of money lent out.
  • Total Collected – cumulative repayments received to date.
Charts
  • The Loan Breakup doughnut chart shows loan distribution by status.
  • Bar charts give a visual breakdown of loan counts per status category.
Quick Actions

Use the navigation sidebar to jump to Clients, Loans, Staff, Reports, or Settings at any time.

settings Settings

Settings control how the entire system behaves. WARN Incorrect settings can break loan calculations — always double-check before saving. All settings are stored in a single configuration record and take effect immediately on save (no server restart required).

Settings menu location: All settings are accessed via the Settings item in the sidebar. The settings page is divided into tabs: General, Appearance, Date, Loan, Credit Check, Form, Banks, Employers, Roles & Access (also where Staff Accounts — create staff, edit their role/position, reset passwords, and suspend/reactivate access — lives), Credit Assessment, DCC, and T&C and Contracts. Referral commission settings live under Referrers → Settings.
General Settings Tab: General
  • Loan Ref Prefix – short code (up to 5 characters) prepended to every loan reference number (e.g. LMX produces references like LMX-2024-0001).
  • Admin Email Addresses – comma-separated list of addresses that receive system notifications (new applications, defaults, funding alerts).
  • Default From Email – the sender address shown on all outgoing system emails.
  • Support Email – the address displayed on the support/contact pages and used for support-related notifications.
  • Email System to Work Email (Global) – master switch. When ON, system emails (funding, CDN, amortization, messages) can be sent to clients' work email addresses. When OFF, no system email goes to any work email regardless of per-client settings. Individual client overrides still apply when this is ON.
  • Display Decimal Places – choose 0, 1, or 2 decimal places for currency display across the portal and PDF documents (default: 2).
  • Decimal Rounding – rounding method for currency calculations: HALF_UP (standard rounding), CEILING (always rounds up), or FLOOR (always rounds down).
  • Deduction Category Toggles – four independent on/off switches controlling which repayment deduction categories are offered to clients:
    • ALESCO Payroll – government/public servant salary deduction.
    • Private Sector Payroll – private employer salary deduction.
    • Standing Order – automatic bank standing-order deduction.
    • Voluntary Deductions – client-initiated voluntary repayments.
    Only enabled categories appear in the deduction-category dropdown on the loan application form.
  • Company Bank Account (Primary) – Bank name, Account Name, Account Number, Branch, BSB/Sort code, and SWIFT code. Printed on IRSDA and CDN documents. Update here if your bank details change.
  • Alesco Deduction Code – government payroll deduction code used for public servant salary deductions (e.g. LMXCOD).
Appearance Settings (White-Label Branding) Tab: Appearance

These settings let you rebrand the entire portal — colours, logo, and fonts — without touching code. Useful for white-label deployments or refreshing your brand identity.

  • Brand Name – the company/brand name shown in the sidebar header, browser tab titles, and outgoing email subjects (up to 100 characters).
  • Brand Logo – upload your main logo (PNG or SVG recommended). Displayed in the sidebar header and throughout the portal.
  • Login Logo – optional separate logo for the login screen. Falls back to the Brand Logo if not set.
  • Favicon – browser tab icon (ideally 32×32 px PNG). Falls back to the Brand Logo if not set.
  • Primary Color – main brand colour in hex (e.g. #6bb10d). Applied to buttons, links, active states, and accents.
  • Sidebar Background Color – background colour of the left navigation sidebar in hex.
  • Sidebar Light Text – toggle whether sidebar text and icons use a light colour (for dark sidebar backgrounds) or dark colour (for light sidebar backgrounds).
  • Topbar / Report Strip Color – colour of the top navigation strip and report headers in hex.
  • Font Family – choose the system font from: Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Poppins, Inter, Montserrat, Nunito, or Source Sans Pro. Applied portal-wide.
Tip: After changing the logo or colours, hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R) to clear cached assets and see the new branding immediately.
Date Settings Tab: Date
  • Date Format – system-wide date display format. Options: YYYY-MM-DD, DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY, or Jan 31, 2026 (human-readable). Applied to all date fields in the portal, statements, and PDF documents.
Loan Settings Tab: Loan
  • Interest Type – FIXED (single flat rate for all terms), VARIABLE (tiered rates by fortnight bracket), or CUSTOM (custom rate logic).
  • Interest Rate – the rate applied to new loans. For VARIABLE type, tiered rates are used internally based on the fortnight bracket (2 FN = 30%, 3–4 FN = 40%, 5–10 FN = 50%).
  • Loan Amount Min / Max / Increment – defines the range and step of the amount dropdown on the client application form and staff loan creation (e.g. Min K500, Max K5000, Increment K100). Changes take effect immediately on the forms.
  • Fortnight Min / Max / Increment – controls the fortnights range and step (e.g. Min 3, Max 26, Increment 1). Set Max to the highest term available in the repayment table.
  • Processing Fee – enable or disable processing fees on new loans.
  • Processing Fee Collection Mode – CASH (client pays the fee separately) or WITHHELD (fee is deducted from the disbursement amount before payout).
  • Processing Fee Tiers – define tiered processing fees based on loan amount ranges. Each tier has: Min Amount, Max Amount (blank = no upper limit), Fee Amount, and an Active toggle. The system matches the loan amount to the appropriate tier at funding. Add as many tiers as needed.
  • User Loan Application Mode – LOCKED (client selects from preset amount/term combinations using the repayment table) or OPEN_REPAYMENT (client enters a custom repayment amount; available only for clients with a max_loan_amount set on their profile).
  • Staff Loan Creation Mode – LOCKED (staff uses the repayment table) or OPEN_REPAYMENT (staff enters a custom repayment amount manually).
  • Refinance Type – determines how additional loans are structured against an existing running loan:
    • OFFSET_BALANCE – new loan amount offsets the current running balance; the remaining difference is paid to the client; old loan is closed.
    • ADD_ON – new loan amount and interest are added to the existing balance; the term is extended; a single combined repayment is calculated.
    • CONCURRENT – both loans run side by side; repayment is doubled during the overlap period, then steps down to the new loan's repayment once the first loan's term ends.
    • ADD_ON_VARIED – like Add On (old balance + new loan combined), but instead of the system deriving the term and repayment automatically, staff enter the combined term and fortnightly repayment by hand on the funding form.
    • REFINANCE_NOT_ALLOWED STRICT – disables refinancing entirely. A client with any existing loan (funded or pending) cannot get a new or additional loan under any circumstances, staff included, until their existing loan is fully Completed. Enforces one loan per client.
  • Send Cash Disbursement Notice – when ON, a CDN PDF is emailed to the client automatically when a loan is funded.
  • CDN Send to Payroll Officer – when ON, the CDN is also emailed to the client's payroll officer (the employer's payroll section email).
  • Send Loan Amortization Schedule – when ON, the LAS is emailed to the client (as an HTML table in the email body) when a loan is funded.
  • Display Referrer on Loan – when ON, the referrer's name is shown on the loan information page (admin and staff views).
  • Send Application Documents – master switch for the application document email system. When ON, the sub-settings below control exactly what is sent and to whom:
    • Send to User – email the application documents to the client.
    • Send to Staff – email the application documents to the assigned staff officer.
    • BCC Admin – BCC the admin email addresses on application document emails.
    • Include Application Form – attach the Loan Application Form PDF.
    • Include Terms & Conditions – attach the Terms & Conditions PDF.
    • Include Statutory Declaration – attach the Statutory Declaration PDF.
    • Include IRSDA – attach the Irrevocable Salary Deduction Authority PDF.
  • Send Alesco Pay Update Email – when ON, an email is sent to the client when an Alesco payroll deduction is confirmed against their loan.
Loan Amortization Schedule (LAS) Column Settings

Control which columns appear on the Loan Amortization Schedule PDF. Turn off columns you don't need to produce a cleaner, narrower document.

  • Show Period – fortnight period number (1, 2, 3…).
  • Show Repayment Date – the scheduled repayment date for each period.
  • Show Opening Balance – balance at the start of each period.
  • Show Interest – interest portion of each repayment.
  • Show Principal – principal portion of each repayment.
  • Show Repayment – total repayment amount (interest + principal).
  • Show Closing Balance – balance at the end of each period.
Credit Check Settings Tab: Credit Check
  • Automatic Credit Check – YES applies auto approve/decline rules based on the credit threshold; NO means the admin decides manually on every application.
  • Approval Credit Threshold – the minimum credit rating a client must have for automatic approval (e.g. 80.00). Clients below this threshold are flagged for manual review.
  • Percentage of Gross Allowable – maximum repayment as a percentage of gross salary (e.g. 30 means a client's total fortnightly repayments cannot exceed 30% of their gross fortnightly pay). Used to calculate each client's repayment limit.
  • Default Repayment Limit – the repayment limit assigned to a client by default when their account is first activated (before job/income details are filled in).
  • Minimum Repayment Limit – a global floor for repayment limits. No client's repayment limit can be set below this value, even if their income is very low.
  • Repayment Limit Check Enabled – when ON, the system enforces the repayment limit on new loan applications (rejects applications where the repayment would exceed the limit). When OFF, the limit is shown as a warning but applications are still accepted.
  • Bypass Activation Check – when ON, clients can apply for loans without their account being fully activated. Use with caution — normally leave OFF so only activated clients can apply.
  • Default Interest Type – PERCENTAGE (charges a % of the missed repayment amount) or FIXED (charges a flat kina amount per default event).
  • Default Interest Rate / Amount – enter the percentage (e.g. 20 for 20%) or the flat kina amount (e.g. 50 for K50.00) depending on the type selected.
  • Default Interest Calculation Mercy Days – number of days after the missed payment date before the automatic classifier fires (e.g. 7 means the system waits 7 days before classifying the loan as defaulted).
  • Default Interest Base – determines what the default interest is calculated on: SHORTFALL (interest charged only on the missed/shortfall amount) or FULL (interest charged on the full scheduled repayment amount).
  • Auto-Default on Shortfall – when ON, a partial payment that is below the scheduled amount automatically triggers a default on the shortfall portion.
  • Automatic Default Classification – when ON, the Celery scheduled task automatically calculates and applies default interest, updates the loan balance, creates a statement entry, and emails the client once the mercy period elapses with no payment recorded. When OFF, defaults must be registered manually via the loan view.
Form Settings Tab: Form

Control which fields appear on client-facing and staff-facing forms. Each field can be independently enabled/disabled and marked as required or optional.

  • Personal Information form – toggle and set required status for: first name, middle name, last name, gender, date of birth, marital status, profile photo.
  • Contact & Address form – mobile 1, mobile 2, residential address, residential province, place of origin, province, resident owner status.
  • Bank Account form – bank, bank branch, account name, account number, bank standing order upload.
  • User Uploads form – NID, passport, driver's licence, superannuation ID, work ID (each with its number field).
  • Employer Information form – sector, employer, organisation type, office address, payroll officer name/phone/email.
  • Job Information form – job title, start date, pay frequency, last pay date, gross pay, net pay, employee file number.
  • Payment form – payment date, amount, mode, statement description.
  • Employer Preregistration Required – when ON, the employer dropdown on client and staff forms only shows pre-registered employers; all payroll officer details are auto-filled server-side from the employer record. When OFF, all employer and payroll officer fields are shown as editable inputs.
  • Default Payment Description – the default text used for the payment description field when it is disabled or left empty (e.g. "Fortnightly repayment").
Note: Fields disabled here are hidden from both client and staff forms, but data already saved in those fields is not deleted. Re-enabling a field restores it with its previous data intact.
Bank Settings Tab: Banks
  • Bank Management – add, edit, activate/deactivate banks available in the client bank-account dropdown. Each bank stores: Name, SWIFT code, BSB prefix, and Active status.
  • Bank Branch Management – add branches under each bank. Each branch stores: Parent bank, Branch name, BSB number, Address, City, Province, and Active status. When a client selects a bank, the branch dropdown automatically filters to show only that bank's active branches.
Tip: Deactivate (don't delete) banks or branches that are no longer used. This preserves historical loan data that references them while hiding them from new forms.
Employer Settings Tab: Employers
  • Register approved employer organisations. Each record stores: Name, Sector (PUBLIC/PRIVATE/SOE/SME), Organisation Type (DEPARTMENT/SOE/COMPANY/MSME), Office Address, Payroll Officer Name, Payroll Officer Phone, and Payroll Officer Email.
  • Employer Preregistration – when ON (Form Settings), the employer dropdown only shows pre-registered employers; payroll officer details auto-fill from the employer record. When OFF, all fields are manual.
  • Payroll Contacts Directory – available under Employers → Payroll Contacts. Shows all registered employers with payroll officer details in a searchable DataTable. Export as PDF (landscape A4) or Excel.
  • Employer Overview – aggregate view showing loan counts, outstanding balances, and collection rates per employer.
  • Loans by Employer – detailed report of all loans grouped by employer, exportable as Excel, TXT, or PDF.
Referral Settings Referrers → Settings
  • Referral Commission – the fixed kina amount paid to a referrer for each referred client (e.g. K50.00). Recorded against each referral at the time the client is linked; changing the rate later does not affect referrals already recorded.
  • Referral Payment Day – the day of the month on which commission payments are processed (default: 31, meaning end of month).
Location Management Settings → Locations
  • Manage branch/office locations. Each location stores: Name, Province (22 PNG provinces), Address, Email, Phone.
  • Locations are used for filtering clients, loans, and transactions by branch, and for location-based reporting.
  • Each location tracks live metrics: loan count, defaults, funded amount, interest, repayments, arrears, outstanding, recovery count, client count, clients with loans, clients in recovery.
Important: Changing interest rates, loan limits, or default settings affects new loans only. Existing running loans retain the terms set at their approval time. Always test setting changes during off-hours and verify a sample loan calculation after saving.

account_balance Managing Loans

All loan lifecycle management — from application review to disbursement and repayment tracking — happens here. Start at Loans in the sidebar, or jump straight to the All Loans list.

Loan Lists (Tabs)

The All Loans page has a row of tabs across the top — each is its own filtered list:

  • All Loans – every loan regardless of status.
  • Pending Loans – applications awaiting review.
  • Running Loans – disbursed, repayments active.
  • Default Loans – missed repayments beyond grace period.
  • In Recovery – loans in the collections process.
  • Completed Loans – fully repaid and closed.
  • Awaiting Refund – see the Loan Credits & Refunds section in the sidebar; loans settled in full where the client overpaid and is still owed money back.
Reviewing an Application
1
Go to Loans → Pending.
2
Open the loan. Review details, employer, amount, repayment schedule, and documents.
3
Check the Credit Summary for existing loans and debt-to-income ratio.
4
Click Approve or Reject.
Disbursing (Funding)
1
Confirm all signed documents are uploaded.
2
Click Fund Loan. Status moves to Running.
3
A Cash Disbursement Notice PDF is generated automatically (if enabled in Loan Settings).
Loan Amount Limits (Min / Max)
  • Every loan-creation path — the client's own application, staff Create Loan, Add Existing Loan with Details, and Add Additional Loan — checks the amount against the global Loan Amount Min/Max (Settings → Loan).
  • A client can have a personal override on their profile (Max Loan Amount) which replaces the global maximum for that client only — set it higher for a trusted client, or lower to cap them below the normal limit.
  • An amount below the minimum or above the effective maximum is rejected immediately with a clear error, before anything is created.
Existing Loan Guard
  • When staff or admin tries to create a new loan for a client who already has an active or pending loan, the system redirects to an Existing Loan page instead of the creation form.
  • The page shows the client's current loan details (ref, amount, outstanding balance, status, next payment date).
  • If the intent is to add funds on top of the existing balance, click Get Additional Loan — this takes you to the Additional Loan creation form which handles the balance transfer automatically (unless Refinance Not Allowed is set — see below).
Additional Loans & Refinancing
  • Use Add Additional Loan (also on the Custom Functions page — see sidebar) when a client with a running loan needs more funds. The system closes the old loan, transfers the outstanding balance to the new loan, and recalculates the repayment schedule according to the configured Refinance Type (Settings → Loan): Offset Balance, Add On, Concurrent, Add On (Varied), or Refinance Not Allowed.
  • NEW When Refinance Type is set to Refinance Not Allowed, a client with any existing loan (funded or still pending) cannot get a new or additional loan at all — not even staff can override this. The Get Additional Loan button is hidden on the Existing Loan page, funding an approved application for a client who still has a running loan is blocked outright, and a new loan only becomes possible once their existing one reaches Completed. This enforces a strict one-loan-per-client policy.
  • A Cash Disbursement Notice and Amortization Schedule are sent at disbursement even for additional loans.
Apply Advance to Missed Payments NEW
  • When a client has a prepaid Advance Balance (shown on the loan page) large enough to fully cover a missed repayment, clicking Create Default is blocked with a warning — a default should never be charged when the money to cover it is already sitting there.
  • Instead, use the Apply Advance to Missed Payments button (shown on the loan page whenever the client has an advance balance). It settles the earliest overdue repayment straight from the advance balance and posts a clean statement line — no default, no interest charged.
  • If the advance only partly covers the missed repayment, Create Default still works as normal: it nets off whatever advance exists and charges default interest on the remaining shortfall only.
Recording Repayments
  • Click Add Payment, enter date and amount.
  • Bulk payments can be uploaded via Upload Payments (CSV/Excel).
Loan Statement & Amortization Schedule
  • Full transaction history is on the Statement tab. Click Download Statement for a PDF.
  • The LAS tab shows a projected repayment table for the full loan term.
  • Regenerate Dates (on the loan page) rebuilds the remaining repayment schedule from a new next-payment date, term, and frequency — use it when a client's pay cycle changes or the original schedule was mis-anchored. Already-settled history is kept untouched.
volunteer_activism Loan Credits & Refunds NEW

When a closing payment on a loan overpays the outstanding balance — most commonly an external refinance payoff that turns out to be more than what was actually owed — the excess is never silently discarded. It is tracked as a credit owed back to the client until it is refunded or deliberately written off against the account.

How a Loan Ends Up "Awaiting Refund"
  • A payment comes in that clears the loan's balance in full and then some — e.g. another finance company pays off the client's loan, but the payoff figure they send is a little higher than the true balance.
  • The loan is marked Awaiting Refund (its own tab on the All Loans page — see Awaiting Refund directly) instead of Completed. The debt itself is fully settled; only the refund is outstanding.
  • The exact excess amount is recorded as a Client Credit against that client, visible in the Client Credits register.
The Client Credits Register
1
Go to Client Credits in the sidebar (under Transactions).
2
The Owing to Clients table lists every credit not yet refunded: client name, loan reference, amount, reason, and date created.
3
Once you have actually paid the client back (bank transfer, cash, cheque), find their row and use the Mark Refunded form: enter the date refunded, an optional note (e.g. a bank transfer reference), and upload the refund receipt as proof.
4
Marking a credit refunded automatically finalises the loan to Completed if it was still sitting in Awaiting Refund — you don't need a separate step.
5
The Refunded table below keeps a permanent record of every completed refund, including the uploaded receipt. Export the whole register as CSV using the button in the top-right of the page.
Attribute to Client Credit & Close Now
  • Sometimes you want to close the loan out immediately and deal with the refund separately later (e.g. the amount is small, or the client hasn't confirmed how they want it paid back yet).
  • Open the loan itself — the Awaiting Refund banner at the top has an Attribute to Client Credit & Close Now button. This finalises the loan to Completed straight away, while the credit stays open and owing in the Client Credits register until it's refunded whenever convenient.
Nothing is ever lost: every credit is tied to both the client and the specific loan it came from, and the register keeps a full history of what was owed, when it was created, when it was refunded, and the receipt proving it — even after the loan itself is Completed and archived.
badge Staff Management, Roles & Access

Staff (loan officers) can view clients and loans, record payments, and manage their pipeline. All staff accounts, roles, and access control live under Settings → Roles & Access.

Adding, Editing & Suspending a Staff Member
1
Go to Settings → Roles & Access.
2
Click Add Staff. Enter name, email, and a temporary password, and pick their Role and Level.
3
To edit an existing staff member's role or details, or to reset their password, find them in the staff list and click Edit / Reset Password.
4
To temporarily remove access (without deleting the account), click Suspend. A suspended account is blocked from logging in until reactivated. Click Reactivate to restore access — you cannot suspend your own account.
Roles
  • Staff – the standard loan-officer role: can view/update loans and clients, record payments, but cannot approve/reject loans or access Settings, Reports, or the Admin Dashboard.
  • Manager – an elevated staff role with additional workflow permissions (e.g. committing payments, credit-assessment sign-off) beyond a standard Staff account, without full admin/superuser access.
Credit Assessment
  • Settings → Credit Assessment configures the extended credit-assessment workflow: statement of position, referee details, and previous-employer history collected from applicants for a fuller picture of their financial standing before approval.
Terms & Conditions / Contracts
  • Settings → T&C and Contracts manages the legal contract templates used for signed loan agreements, including generation and tracking of signed-contract uploads.
DCC Credit Bureau Settings
  • Settings → DCC configures the automatic credit-bureau scoring integration: whether a benchmark score is fetched automatically on application, whether it's used to auto-scale a client's repayment limit and maximum loan ceiling, and the minimum score below which an application is automatically declined. See the DCC Integration section for the data-feed side of this.
business Employers & Payroll Contacts

The Employers module manages the organisations that employ your clients. It controls the employer dropdown on application forms and maintains a central payroll contact directory.

Registering an Employer
1
Go to Settings → Employer Settings.
2
Click Add Employer. Fill in: Name, Sector (Public / Private / SOE / SME), Organisation Type, Office Address.
3
Enter the Payroll Officer Name, Payroll Officer Phone, and Payroll Officer Email. These are shared across all clients at the same employer — one set of payroll contact details per organisation.

Employer Preregistration
  • Controlled by Form Settings → Employer Preregistration Required.
  • When ON: client and staff employer forms show only an employer dropdown. Selecting an employer auto-fills all payroll officer details server-side — nothing is displayed or editable by the user. The data is saved silently.
  • When OFF: all employer and payroll officer fields are shown as editable inputs on the form.
  • TIP Preregistration prevents inconsistent payroll officer data across clients at the same employer.
Work Email vs Payroll Officer Email
  • Work Email — unique to the client; their personal work email address. Used for individual notifications.
  • Payroll Officer Email — the shared email for the employer's payroll section. The same email appears on every client from that employer. Used for CDN and amortization schedule delivery to the payroll officer.
  • These are stored in separate fields and never conflict.
Employer Overview & Loans by Employer
  • Employers → Overview — summary statistics per employer: total loans, total repayments, total outstanding, defaults, arrears.
  • Employers → Loans by Employer — lists all active loans grouped by employer. Useful for preparing payroll deduction schedules.
Payroll Contacts Directory
  • Employers → Payroll Contacts — a DataTable listing every registered employer with their payroll officer's name, phone, email, office address, client count, and active status.
  • Use the search bar to find a specific organisation quickly.
  • Export the full list as a formatted PDF (A4 landscape) or Excel (.xlsx) using the buttons in the top-right corner of the card. The export filename includes the current date.
Best practice: Keep payroll officer details up to date in the employer record. When a payroll officer changes, update the employer record — all existing and future client profiles linked to that employer will automatically use the new contact details on all outgoing emails.

receipt_long Alesco Update

Alesco is the government payroll system. The Alesco Update tool (Transactions → Alesco Update) reads the periodic Deduction Variation Report, matches every employee deduction to a client's loan, and posts the repayments in one review-and-confirm flow — with full cancel and rollback support if a file was wrong.

1. Uploading a Deduction File
1
Go to Transactions → Alesco Update and choose the Alesco Deduction Variation Report text file.
2
Click Process. The system reads the Period End date, paycode and every employee's This Period deduction, creates an upload (Pay Run) record, and matches each line to a client by Employee File Number.
3
You land on the Confirm Payments page showing every line with This Period vs the loan's Expected Repayment and the variance.

The Recent Uploads table below the upload form lists past uploads (sortable/searchable) with their status and actions.

2. Confirming Payments
  • Confirm (per line) – posts that one deduction as a repayment: payment record, statement entry, balance update, and client notification (per Loan Settings).
  • Confirm All – batch-confirms every pending matched line. Use this for a clean file.
  • Short deductions are handled per your Credit Check settings (shortfall default); over-deductions become advance credit.
3. Line Statuses
  • Pending – matched, awaiting confirmation.
  • Confirmed – payment posted; the action button changes to Rollback.
  • Unmatched – the Employee File Number matched no client. Fix the file number on the client profile, then re-upload or confirm manually.
  • Skipped – the listing shows a zero deduction for this pay (no money came in), so there is nothing to confirm. The expected repayment still falls due and can be defaulted from the loan page if unpaid.
4. Cancel Update

On the Confirm page, Cancel Update abandons the whole upload so you can fix and re-upload the file:

  • If nothing was confirmed yet, the upload and its pending lines are simply deleted.
  • If the run was partially confirmed, every confirmed payment is rolled back first (see below) and the pending lines are discarded, then the upload is deleted.
5. Rollback (wrong upload confirmed)

Every confirmation snapshots the loan first, so it can be reversed exactly:

  • Per line: on the Confirm page, click Rollback next to a confirmed line. The payment and its statement entries are removed and the loan balance, arrears, schedule position and status are restored to exactly what they were before.
  • Whole upload: on the Alesco Update page, the most recent completed/partial upload shows a Rollback button. It reverses every confirmed payment in that run, discards any pending lines, and removes the upload — a clean slate to re-upload the corrected file.
Safety rule: a line will refuse to roll back if any newer payment or statement was recorded on that loan after the confirmation — rolling back would erase that later work. Those loans are listed in the error message and must be reversed manually. Only the latest upload offers run-level rollback for the same reason.

WARN Payments confirmed before rollback support was added have no snapshot and cannot be auto-reversed — correct those via the loan page.

6. Categorized Unmatched Payments NEW

A deduction line that couldn't be matched to any loan doesn't just disappear — it lands on the Unmatched Payment Listing (also reachable from Reports → Alesco Payroll Reports → Unmatched Payments — Categorized), which now sorts every unmatched line into one of four reasons so you know exactly what to do with it:

  • No Client Record – the employee file number in the payroll file doesn't match any client at all.
  • Possible Typo – no exact match, but the system found a client with a very similar file number and suggests it as the likely correct one — one click links the payment to that client.
  • Ambiguous Match – more than one client shares that file number; pick the correct one from the candidates shown.
  • Client Has No Loan – the file number matched a real client, but they have no loan for the deduction to apply to.

Use the Link to Client action on a row to correct the file number and re-match it — the line immediately becomes payable on the normal Confirm page, with no separate "sync" step needed. Once money is confirmed unmatched-but-received from a non-client, the same page tracks it as owed back to them, with a Mark Refunded action and CSV export, exactly like the Client Credits register described earlier.

Duplicate upload warning: if the same pay period was uploaded twice for the same payroll/paycode, a banner appears on the Unmatched page flagging it — cancel the repeat upload from the Alesco Update page before confirming further lines, or you risk double-posting the same deductions.

The system generates pre-filled PDF documents from client and loan data.

Documents Available

  • Terms & Conditions / Loan Agreement – borrower name, amounts, all legal clauses.

build Custom Functions

Administrative tools for data management and bulk operations, at Custom Functions.

  • Add New Loan – create a brand new, fully-detailed loan for a client one at a time (amount, term, dates entered by hand). Blocked if the client already has an active or pending loan (see Existing Loan Guard in the Loans section).
  • Add Additional Loan – give a client with a running loan an additional/refinanced loan, structured per the configured Refinance Type. Disabled entirely when Refinance Type is Refinance Not Allowed.
  • Upload New Loans – import historical loan data from Excel to migrate an existing portfolio.
  • Direct Loan Update – update loan fields in bulk via an Excel template.
Staff have their own consolidated hub for exactly this kind of data entry — the staff portal's Existing Loan Functions page bundles Add New Loan, Add Additional Loan, bulk loan/statement uploads, and per-loan document upload in one place, with a brief explanation on each button.

WARN Bulk uploads are irreversible. Test with a small batch first and keep a data backup before running large uploads.

schedule Automation & Background Jobs

The system runs scheduled background jobs that handle reminders, defaults, and classification automatically — no manual action needed for routine operations. These are powered by a background worker (Celery) and a scheduler (Celery beat) backed by Redis.

Scheduled Jobs & What They Do
  • Repayment Reminders – run daily (around 8:50 AM) to email clients with repayments coming due.
  • Run Defaults – runs every 30 minutes to flag loans whose repayment is overdue beyond the grace period, add default interest, and notify the client.
  • Default Classification – runs every 10 minutes to keep aging/recovery classifications up to date.
  • Payments Expected Today – runs regularly to track repayments due on the current day.
  • Bulk Messaging – when you send a bulk email/message it is processed in the background so the page returns immediately.
How to Tell It's Working
  • Clients receive reminder and default emails without staff triggering them.
  • On the server, the worker and scheduler run as services: loanmasta-celery and loanmasta-celerybeat (with redis-server).
  • Activity is logged to logs/celery-worker.log and logs/celery-beat.log.
If Automation Stops
  • You can still run things manually: Functions → Run Defaults and Send Repayment Reminder.
  • Ask your server administrator to confirm the redis-server, loanmasta-celery, and loanmasta-celerybeat services are active.

TIP Bulk messaging still works even if the background worker is briefly unavailable — it falls back to sending within the request, so notices are never silently lost.

hub DCC Credit-Bureau Integration

The system exposes a secure data feed (clients, loans, statements) consumed by the external DCC credit-bureau service.

How It Works
  • The DCC client pulls eligible records through the API and the system marks them as sent so they are not duplicated.
  • Only clients who have given credit consent are included in the shared profile feed.
Security
  • The feed is protected by a secret API key. The DCC client must send this key with every request, or access is refused.
  • The key lives in the server's environment configuration — never in the codebase. If the key is rotated, the DCC client must be updated with the new value.

WARN If the DCC sync stops returning data, first confirm the DCC client is sending the correct API key before investigating further.

support_agent Support Ticket System

The support ticket system lets clients raise issues directly through the portal. Admins manage and respond to all tickets from the admin panel.

Accessing Support Tickets

Go to Support → Tickets in the admin sidebar. This opens the main ticket list with live stats at the top showing total, awaiting reply, replied, open, and closed counts.

Ticket Statuses
  • Awaiting Reply – client has sent a message and is waiting for a response from admin.
  • Replied – admin has responded; ball is in the client's court.
  • Closed – ticket has been resolved and closed by admin or the client.
Filtering Tickets

Use the filter tabs at the top of the ticket list — All, Awaiting Reply, Replied, Open, Closed — or click the stat cards to jump to that filtered view. The orange badge next to Awaiting Reply shows the count of tickets needing attention.

Viewing & Replying to a Ticket
1
Click View on any ticket row to open the ticket thread.
2
The left sidebar shows the ticket metadata: ref, category, subject, status, client details, employer, and timestamps. Use View Client Profile to jump directly to the client record.
3
The right panel shows the full conversation thread. Client messages appear on the left in blue; admin replies appear on the right in green.
4
Type your reply in the text box at the bottom and click Send Reply. An email notification is automatically sent to the client.
5
Optionally attach a file to your reply using the Attach file link.
Closing a Ticket
1
Once the issue is resolved, click Close & Resolve Ticket in the sidebar.
2
The system posts a system note to the thread and emails the client confirming resolution.
3
If a ticket was closed prematurely, click Reopen Ticket to restore it to active status.
Ticket Categories
  • Loan Requirements – questions about eligibility, documents needed.
  • Pending Loan – follow-up on a submitted application.
  • Loan Repayment – queries about payment amounts, dates, or missed payments.
  • Loan Issue – problems with a running or completed loan.
  • Account Activation – client cannot log in or account is inactive.
  • Web App Usage – technical difficulties using the portal.
Best practice: Aim to respond to all Awaiting Reply tickets within one business day. Clients receive an email for every reply, so keep responses clear and professional. Close tickets only after the client's issue is fully resolved.
What Clients See
  • Clients access their tickets from Dashboard → Support Center.
  • They can create a new ticket, view the full conversation thread, reply, attach files, and mark their own ticket as resolved.
  • Their view shows status cards (Total, Awaiting Reply, Replied, Closed) and a list of all their tickets sorted by most recent activity.
Email Notifications Triggered
  • When a client creates a ticket → admin receives an email with the ticket content.
  • When a client replies → admin receives an email notification.
  • When admin replies → client receives an email with the reply text and a link to view the ticket.
  • When a ticket is closed → client receives a resolution confirmation email.
share Referrer Management & Commission

The referral system lets you register individuals who refer clients to you, automatically track the commission owed, and mark payments at the end of each month.

How It Works – Overview
  • You register a referrer (name, contact, bank details) once.
  • When a staff member creates a new client, they select the referrer from the Referred By dropdown on the client creation form.
  • The system immediately creates a referral record and assigns the fixed commission amount (set in Referral Settings) to that referrer.
  • At end of month, you review outstanding commissions and mark them paid — the system records who paid and when.
Referral Settings
1
Go to Settings → Referral Settings.
2
Set the Referral Commission (K) — the fixed kina amount paid per referred client who registers.
3
Set the Referral Payment Day — the day of the month commissions are paid out (e.g. 31 = last day of month).
4
Click Update Referral Settings. The new rate applies to all future referral records from this point.
Note: Changing the commission rate does not retroactively update existing referral records. Each record stores the commission amount that was active at the time the client was referred.
Adding a Referrer
1
Go to Referrers → Add Referrer in the admin sidebar.
2
Enter the referrer's full name, phone number, and email address.
3
Enter their bank account details (Bank, Branch, Account Name, Account Number) — these are used when paying commission.
4
Set status to Active. Only Active referrers appear in the client creation dropdown.
5
Click Add Referrer. The referrer is now available for staff to select when registering new clients.
Viewing a Referrer's Profile
  • Click any referrer in the list to open their profile page.
  • The profile shows: total clients referred, total commission earned, total unpaid, and total paid out to date.
  • Referred clients are grouped by month, showing the commission amount for each, and whether it has been paid.
  • Each monthly group has a Mark Paid button to record payment for that period.
Commission Overview (Monthly View)
1
Go to Referrers → Commission Overview.
2
Use the month selector (top right) to pick the month you want to process.
3
The page shows every referrer who has referrals in that month, with their total commission and a breakdown of each referred client.
4
Once you have transferred the money to the referrer's bank account, click Mark Paid next to their name. The system records the payment with your name and today's date.
5
Paid records show a green ✓ All Paid badge. Unpaid ones show an orange Unpaid badge with the outstanding count.
Deactivating a Referrer
  • Open the referrer's profile and click Deactivate. They will no longer appear in the client creation dropdown.
  • Existing referral records and unpaid commissions are not affected — they remain visible and payable.
  • Reactivate at any time by clicking Activate on their profile.
Referrer Stats at a Glance (List Page)
  • The referrers list shows each referrer's total referral count and their current unpaid commission balance.
  • Summary cards at the top show portfolio-wide totals: active referrers, all-time referrals, total unpaid, and total paid out.

help A client submitted an application but I cannot see it in Pending — where is it? expand_more
Check the All Loans table and search by name or email. The loan may have been actioned into a different status by another admin. Confirm the client completed the final submit step — partial applications stay in draft.

Ask the client to check spam/junk. If nothing is there, open the loan view page and click Resend Documents Email. Ensure the client's email address on their profile is correct first.

The system permits multiple loans per client. The credit check will include the existing loan balance in the debt-to-income calculation. Approval is at admin discretion based on the credit summary.
payments Repayments & Payments
help A payment was entered with the wrong amount — how do I correct it? expand_more
Go to the loan's Statement tab, find the entry, and click Delete. Then re-enter the correct payment with Add Payment. Only admins can delete statement entries.
help The loan is showing as Defaulted but the client has made all payments — why? expand_more
A payment was likely posted with an incorrect date, or Run Defaults detected a gap. Review the statement to confirm dates. If everything is correct, use Update Loan Status to manually set the status back to Running.
help How does bulk payment upload handle partial payments? expand_more
Each row is processed independently. Partial payments reduce the balance and the system flags arrears if the total for a period is below the scheduled instalment. Enter the exact amount received — no automatic rounding.

settings Settings & Configuration
help I updated the interest rate — will it affect existing running loans? expand_more
No. The Settings rate applies to new applications only. Running loans keep the rate set at approval time. Edit the loan directly (admin only) if a correction is needed.
help What does "Run Defaults" do and when should I use it? expand_more
Run Defaults scans Running loans for missed repayments past the mercy period and classifies them as Defaulted, applying default interest. If Automatic Default Classification is ON in Credit Settings, this runs on a Celery schedule automatically. Use the manual Run Defaults button only if the scheduler has not run, or right after a large batch payment upload.
help The loan amount slider on the client application form shows the wrong range — how do I fix it? expand_more
Go to Settings → Loan Settings and update Loan Amount Min, Loan Amount Max, Fortnight Min, and Fortnight Max. These values feed directly into the slider on the application form — no code change or server restart is needed.
help I want to stop system emails going to clients' work email addresses globally — how? expand_more
Go to Settings → General Settings and turn off Enable System Emails to Client Work Email. This is a global switch — when OFF, no system email (loan funding, CDN, amortization, messages) will be sent to any client's work email regardless of per-client settings.
help Where did the Default Interest Rate and Mercy Days settings go? They're not in Loan Settings anymore. expand_more
These settings were moved to Settings → Credit Check Settings where they sit alongside the Automatic Default Classification toggle. You'll find: Default Interest Type, Default Interest Rate/Amount, Default Mercy Period (days), Enable Automatic Default Classification.
help What is the difference between Payroll Officer Email and Work Email on a client profile? expand_more
Work Email is the client's own personal work email — unique to each client and used for direct notifications. Payroll Officer Email is the shared email for the employer's payroll section — the same address appears on all clients from the same employer and is used for CDN and amortization schedule delivery to payroll. They are stored in separate fields and never conflict.
help How do I rebrand the portal with my own logo and colours? expand_more
Go to Settings → Appearance. Upload your logo (Brand Logo and optionally a separate Login Logo and Favicon), set the Primary Color, Sidebar Background Color, Topbar Color, and choose a Font Family. Changes take effect immediately. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R) to clear cached assets.
help How do I set up tiered processing fees based on loan amount? expand_more
In Settings → Loan Settings, enable Processing Fee and choose the collection mode (Cash or Withheld). Then add tiers: each tier has a Min Amount, Max Amount (leave blank for no upper limit), and Fee Amount. The system automatically selects the matching tier when a loan is funded based on the loan amount.
help Which columns should I show on the Loan Amortization Schedule? expand_more
In Settings → Loan Settings, under the LAS Column Settings section, toggle each column on or off. For a standard client-facing schedule, show Period, Repayment Date, Repayment, and Closing Balance. For an internal/audit version, enable all columns including Opening Balance, Interest, and Principal.
help How do I control which documents are emailed with a loan application? expand_more
In Settings → Loan Settings, turn on Send Application Documents (the master switch), then use the sub-toggles to choose: Send to User, Send to Staff, BCC Admin, and which PDFs to attach (Application Form, Terms & Conditions, Statutory Declaration, IRSDA). Each can be toggled independently.
help What is the difference between Offset Balance, Add-On, and Concurrent refinance? expand_more
Set this in Settings → Loan Settings → Refinance Type. Offset Balance pays off the old loan from the new loan amount and gives the client the remainder. Add-On combines both balances into one extended-term loan with a single repayment. Concurrent runs both loans side by side with doubled repayments during the overlap, then steps down. Choose based on your lending policy.
help How do I enable or disable deduction categories (Alesco, Private, Standing Order, Voluntary)? expand_more
In Settings → General Settings, use the four deduction-category toggles. Only enabled categories appear in the deduction dropdown on the loan application form. Disable categories you don't offer to keep the form clean.

share Referrers & Commissions
help How is the referral commission amount determined? expand_more
The commission is a fixed kina amount set in Settings → Referral Settings. Every time a new client is linked to a referrer, the current commission rate is recorded against that referral. Changing the rate later does not affect referrals already recorded.
help A referrer is not appearing in the client creation dropdown — why? expand_more
Only referrers with Active status appear in the dropdown. Open the referrer's profile and click Activate if they have been deactivated. Also ensure the referrer has been registered under Referrers → Add Referrer.
help Can I record a referral for a client who was created before the referral system was set up? expand_more
Not automatically. The referral record is created at the moment a client is registered with a referrer selected. For existing clients, a referral can be added by editing the client's profile and setting the Referred By field — however, the commission record will need to be reviewed manually since it won't have the correct date.
help I accidentally marked a month as paid — can I undo it? expand_more
There is no automatic undo from the UI. To correct this, access the Django admin panel (/appadmin/referral/referralrecord/) and set the affected records back to unpaid. Contact your system administrator if you do not have access to the Django admin panel.
help What happens to unpaid commissions if a referrer is deactivated? expand_more
Deactivating a referrer only prevents them from appearing in new client registrations. All existing referral records, including unpaid commissions, are preserved and remain payable. You can still view and pay them through the referrer's profile or the Commission Overview page.
volunteer_activism Loan Credits & Refunds
help A client's loan disappeared from Completed after a refinance payoff — where did it go? expand_more
If the closing payment overpaid the balance (common with external refinance payoffs), the loan is deliberately kept out of Completed and instead marked Awaiting Refund — its own tab on the All Loans page. The debt is settled, but the excess is still owed to the client and tracked in Client Credits until refunded.
help How do I record that I've refunded a client their excess? expand_more
Go to Client Credits, find their row in the "Owing to Clients" table, and use the Mark Refunded form — enter the date, an optional note, and upload the refund receipt. This automatically finalises the loan to Completed if it was still Awaiting Refund.
help Can I close the loan before actually refunding the client? expand_more
Yes. Open the loan and use Attribute to Client Credit & Close Now on the Awaiting Refund banner. The loan finalises to Completed immediately, and the credit stays open in the Client Credits register until you refund it whenever convenient.
help What does "Refinance Not Allowed" actually block? expand_more
Set in Settings → Loan → Refinance Type. When active, a client with any existing loan (funded or pending) cannot get a new or additional loan at all — Add Additional Loan refuses outright, and funding an approved application for a client who still has a running loan is blocked rather than auto-refinanced. A new loan only becomes possible once their existing one reaches Completed.
help Create Default is refusing to charge a default on a loan with a missed payment — why? expand_more
Check the loan's Advance Balance. If it's large enough to fully cover the missed repayment, the system blocks Create Default (a default should never be charged when the money to cover it already exists) and points you to Apply Advance to Missed Payments instead, which settles the repayment straight from the advance with no default or interest.
