How Referrals Work

The end-to-end referral & commission flow.

  1. Register a referrer under Referrers → Add Referrer with their name, contact, and bank details.
  2. When a staff member creates a new client, they select the referrer from the Referred By dropdown on the client creation form.
  3. The system automatically creates a referral record and assigns the commission amount (set above) to the referrer.
  4. At the end of each month, go to Commission Overview, select the month, and click Mark Paid for each referrer once payment has been made to their bank account.
  5. All historical payment records are stored per referrer and can be viewed on their profile page.
